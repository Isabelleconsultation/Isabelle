Morphopsychology

Morphopsychology is the study of facial structure and features to understand an individual's psychological tendencies and innate personality. Rooted in both morphology and psychology, it provides a unique window into the human psyche.

This section introduces facial zones, bone shapes, and proportions, showing how features correlate with cognitive, emotional, and behavioral patterns. A powerful tool in coaching, education, and self-discovery.
