Graphology

Graphology is the study of handwriting and what it reveals about a person's character, emotions, and psychological state. Each individual's writing style is as unique as a fingerprint and offers rich insight into personality.

You'll explore how stroke pressure, spacing, slant, and letter formations reflect traits like confidence, introversion, anxiety, or leadership. This tool is used in psychology, hiring, forensics, and self-awareness.
